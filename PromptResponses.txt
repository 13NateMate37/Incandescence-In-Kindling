Not quite. pending_change == chosen_budget_value only accepts a change 
that spends the exact budget; it should also accept totals below the budget. 
And subtracting new_score_cost from points_remaining doesn’t account for 
the old score’s cost.

After confirming the proposed total is within the budget, set current_cost 
to pending_change, then recalculate points_remaining as the budget minus 
current_cost.


Use a small while True around the confirmation prompt and its handling. In your current code, the budget check is accidentally under the "n" branch; it needs to run after a "y" response.

Replace the confirmation section with this structure:
while True:
    confirm_change = input("Confirm new Score Y/N?\n").strip().lower()

    if confirm_change == "y":
        old_score_cost = POINTBUY_POINT_COSTS[pointbuy_array[select_AS]]
        new_score_cost = POINTBUY_POINT_COSTS[score_change_to]
        pending_change = current_cost - old_score_cost + new_score_cost

        if pending_change <= chosen_budget_value:
            pointbuy_array[select_AS] = score_change_to
            current_cost = pending_change
            points_remaining = chosen_budget_value - current_cost
        else:
            print("That change is over your budget.")

        break

    elif confirm_change == "n":
        print("Change cancelled.")
        break

    else:
        print("Please enter Y or N.")
